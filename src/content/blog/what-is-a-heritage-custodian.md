You've locked your front door. You've named someone in your will. You've sorted your insurance. But who's looking after your digital memories?

A Heritage Custodian is the answer to a question most people haven't thought to ask: "Who will manage my digital archive when I'm no longer here?"

## The problem it solves

When someone passes away, their digital life usually follows one of two paths:

1. **Everything is lost** - accounts expire, passwords are unknown, files are deleted
2. **Everything is dumped** - a family member inherits a chaotic mess of files with no context or direction

Neither outcome is good. A Heritage Custodian ensures a third path: **your archive is preserved, managed, and passed down intentionally**.

## What a Heritage Custodian is

A Heritage Custodian (HC) is a person you nominate to look after your digital memory archive when you're no longer able to manage it yourself.

They are not:
- An executor (this isn't a legal role)
- A co-owner (they can't access your content while you're alive)
- An editor (they can't change or delete your memories)

They are:
- A guardian of your story
- The person who activates memorial mode when the time comes
- A curator who can add tributes on your behalf
- The bridge between your archive and your family

## How it works

1. **You nominate your HC** - provide their name and email, and optionally write a personal message
2. **They receive an invitation** - a detailed email explaining the role and responsibilities
3. **They create an account** - just like any other user
4. **While you're alive** - they cannot see your private content. They simply know they've been entrusted with this responsibility.
5. **When the time comes** - they submit a memorial mode request
6. **After approval** - your archive transitions to memorial mode:
   - All Legacy-visibility memories become visible to family
   - Sealed time capsules with memorial unlock are opened
   - Your HC becomes a Curator
   - The subscription is cancelled - the archive is preserved permanently at no cost
   - All family members are notified

## What a Curator can do

Once memorial mode is activated, your Heritage Custodian becomes a Curator. They can:

- View all Family and Legacy memories in your archive
- Add new memories (tributes) on your behalf
- Invite additional family members to view the archive

They cannot:
- Edit or delete your existing memories
- Change privacy settings on your content
- Create or modify time capsules
- Delete folders or restructure your archive

Your story stays exactly as you left it. They can only add to it.

## Who to choose

The right Heritage Custodian is someone who:

- **You trust completely** - they'll have access to your most personal memories
- **Is likely to outlive you** - typically someone younger
- **Is digitally comfortable** - they'll need to use an online platform
- **Understands why this matters** - someone who values family history and memory
- **Is reliable** - they'll need to take action when the time comes

Common choices:
- An adult child
- A younger sibling
- A trusted niece or nephew
- A close family friend

## What if your HC can't fulfil the role?

Life changes. You can update your Heritage Custodian at any time. If your nominated person is no longer suitable - or no longer willing - simply change the nomination in your settings.

## Why it matters

Without a Heritage Custodian:
- Nobody knows your archive exists
- Nobody has permission to manage it
- Your memories may be lost or inaccessible
- Time capsules remain sealed forever
- Legacy content is never revealed to family

With a Heritage Custodian:
- Someone is ready and informed
- The transition is smooth and dignified
- Your family receives your legacy memories
- Time capsules unlock as intended
- Your archive is preserved permanently

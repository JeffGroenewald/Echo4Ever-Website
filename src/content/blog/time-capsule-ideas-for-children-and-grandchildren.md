Imagine your daughter opening a video message from you on her wedding day. Or your grandson reading a letter you wrote when he was born - delivered to him on his 18th birthday. Or your family discovering a sealed collection of photos and stories that only unlocks when you're no longer here.

That's what digital time capsules make possible.

## What is a digital time capsule?

A digital time capsule is a sealed memory - a photo, video, letter, or collection - that stays locked until a specific moment arrives. Once sealed, nobody can see it. Not even you.

When the moment comes, the capsule opens automatically and the person you chose receives it.

## Three types of time capsule unlock

### 1. On a specific date

Set an exact date and the capsule unlocks automatically when that day arrives.

**Perfect for:**
- Your child's 18th or 21st birthday
- A future wedding anniversary
- The day your grandchild starts school
- Christmas Day, five years from now
- A retirement date

### 2. At a personal milestone

Describe the event and unlock it yourself when the milestone happens.

**Perfect for:**
- "When you graduate from university"
- "When you buy your first house"
- "When you have your first child"
- "When you finally visit the country our family came from"

### 3. After memorial activation

The capsule stays sealed until your Heritage Custodian activates memorial mode. This happens when you're no longer here.

**Perfect for:**
- Final messages to your family
- The full story of your life - told in your own words
- Photos and memories you want revealed only after you're gone
- Wisdom, advice, or secrets you want shared at the right time

## Time capsule ideas by recipient

### For your children

- **A letter written the day they were born** - What you felt, what you hoped for them, what the world was like that day
- **A video tour of their childhood home** - Walk through the rooms, explain what happened where
- **Your favourite photos of them growing up** - With captions explaining what you remember about each moment
- **Life advice for their 18th birthday** - Things you wish someone had told you
- **Your recipe collection** - The dishes they grew up eating, written in your handwriting or recorded in your voice

### For your grandchildren

- **"Who I am" introduction** - For grandchildren who might not remember you, or who haven't been born yet
- **Family history they don't know** - Where the family came from, what your parents were like, traditions that shouldn't be forgotten
- **A video message** - Just you, talking to them. Your face, your voice, your personality. That's enough.
- **Photos of their parent as a child** - With stories they've never heard

### For your partner

- **Love letters for future anniversaries** - Seal one for your 30th, 40th, 50th anniversary
- **"Remember when" photo collection** - Your favourite moments together with notes about why they mattered
- **A message for after you're gone** - The thing you'd want them to know if you couldn't say it in person

### For yourself

- **A letter to your future self** - Set it for 5 or 10 years from now. What are you worried about? What are you proud of? What do you hope will be different?
- **Goals and predictions** - Seal them and see how accurate you were
- **A snapshot of today** - What does your daily life look like right now? You'll be amazed how much changes.

## Tips for creating meaningful time capsules

1. **Be personal, not perfect** - A slightly rambling video of you being yourself is worth more than a polished script
2. **Include your voice** - If you can record audio or video instead of just writing, do it. Voices are the first thing we forget.
3. **Give context** - Don't just include a photo. Explain who, when, where, and why.
4. **Don't overthink it** - A single photo with three sentences of context is a complete time capsule. You don't need to create a documentary.
5. **Write the unlock message** - When the capsule opens, the recipient sees a message. Use it. "Happy 18th birthday - I wrote this the day you were born."
